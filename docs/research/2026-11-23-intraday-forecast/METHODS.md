# Evidence, scoring and reproduction

**Dates and populations.** All event clocks use America/New_York. The retrospective includes issued November 16–18 briefs, six-month reconstructions, comparisons trained January–August, the later September 1–November 18 block and a separate November 20 IWM replay. A calendar date can supply up to three ETF-days, two sides and more than one opportunity. The Friday replay is a single session beyond the earlier scoring cutoff. Historical editions and updated-candle variants have different source clocks. Directly issued records, sensor readings at signal time and frozen research reconstructions are identified separately.

**Risk.** For a CALL, signed movement is exit minus entry. For a PUT it is entry minus exit. Divide by the absolute distance between modeled entry and the valid initial stop for straight price R. Each independent exit is a full position from the same entry to one target. A target missed has zero target-hit credit. A stopped position usually has negative exit R. Session-close profit may be positive even when its target was never reached. Serial models evaluate only entries when a position is available. The mean or median of the three counterfactuals describes that signal.

**Clocks and conditions.** A completed 09:39 minute is usable at 09:40. First break means the first settled close to move across a named level in that direction for that session. A wick touch, the first closed break, and a return to the same level can therefore have different timestamps. The 15-minute opening range becomes available at 09:45. Five-minute contextual RVOL uses the completed volume divided by the rolling mean of 50 five-minute bars, including that bar and potentially premarket bars. Native minute RVOL uses its own inputs. A positive directional peer differential in the Friday entry table means that the ETF's same-clock return versus the average of the other two ETFs favored the signal direction. Several indicator dots use related inputs.

**Friday file key.** In the entry ledger `B` denotes the first five eligible native episodes, `C-only` later eligible episodes under an uncapped policy, and `Continuation` or `Reversal` a structural research setup. `R1/R2/R3` are offered R if that price is reached. `ex1/ex2/ex3` are modeled exit R, `t1/t2/t3` are exit clocks and `w1/w2/w3` are reasons. A blank R on 11:13 records zero initial risk. The Friday production alert table uses logged signal prices and printed time limits. It has no modeled entry stop. The actual production records and settled replay include different candidate clocks and market context.

**Reproducibility.** The research companion provides study source, tests, selected full event ledgers, pinned input identities, the reviewed set of local prototype files and their original test receipts where available. The source manifest lists byte lengths and SHA-256. The companion verifier recomputes the paired forecast counts, Friday target summaries, cap and notice counts and the four-way comparison from review tables using Python's standard library. To rerun from private minute bars, restore authorized pinned archives and source as described in the companion. Consumer access to those inputs is a prerequisite for an independent price-by-price reproduction. Historical PDFs, repeated package versions and private vendor or transport payloads are outside the review diff.

**Limits.** Issued archival coverage was incomplete. Reconstructed inputs can differ from original source snapshots. Published studies used multiple event populations and diagnostic variants. A complete native candidate history, including all active live gates and exact intraminute polling, is unavailable for many dates. The brief and candidate strategies predict ETF price movement. No paper-trade outcome here establishes a filled trade or live message delivery. The comparison has no prospective 20-session completion receipt.
