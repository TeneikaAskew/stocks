# Correct point-in-time options dependencies and closing-session capture

## Authorization and intake

This records the scope of the six-stage remediation plan presented after the live AV/GCP audit and authorized by the user on 2026-10-12: "Proceed with the plan, executing inn parallel". Execution method: parallel isolated worktrees, one author and PR per capability. Capability: FEAT-OPTION-001; matching catalog row in docs/product/02-FEATURE-CATALOG.md. The listed issues are touched, not claimed closed without their own acceptance evidence.

## Problem

IWM/SPY October9 feature gamma_flip is NULL while QQQ is populated; this needs source-backed diagnosis rather than fabricated filling. Flow/GEX stores stopped June4–5, and historical earnings options stopped May14 despite a production materialized-view consumer. Earnings T-1 chain logic uses calendar days, which fails Mondays and holidays.

## Non-goals

No expansion to all 128 APIs, change to strategy/model promotion or risk calibration, fabricated financial values, blind rewrite of historical timestamps, unrelated IAM/audience changes, or unbounded backfill. API failures and historical-only exclusions remain explicit.

## Approaches considered

1. Targeted contract repair, independent reviewed streams, bounded source-backed repair and selective deployment — chosen to preserve the verified working paths.
2. Replace the complete ingestion platform — excessive scope and operational risk.
3. Change schedules without repairing data contracts — leaves the demonstrated corruption and observability gaps.

## Design

Trace gamma missingness through prior-session chain, actual crossing, rates and builder joins. Preserve legitimate no-crossing NULLs with explicit availability semantics; repair only a demonstrated parser/join/computation defect and coordinate its freshness expectation. Correct required flow/GEX builders to filter minute interval, valid unexpired contracts and exact prior trading session with explicit gap handling; refresh only bounded consumer-required slices and classify historical-only stores without unnecessary new recurring jobs. Use existing shared exchange calendar for earnings T-1 snapshots. Add event_reported_date provenance to new earnings-chain rows and update earnings_event_outcomes joins to the exact event/session mapping, preserving existing verified legacy coverage. Add bounded --since/--limit recent-event backfill and safe completeness checks. Add shared-calendar realtime RTH guard. Closing captures use deterministic capture_slot_ts=actual NYSE session close plus a unique slot/contract key; snapshot_ts remains the actual retrieval/availability clock. Publish a complete validated ticker chain and its slot-completion marker atomically in one database transaction across bounded chunks; on failure roll back that entire ticker chain. Partial recovery is across whole tickers, not mixed contract retrieval timestamps within one ticker. Completed ticker slots skip duplicate delivery; unfinished tickers can retry and publish a full chain at one actual retrieval timestamp. Existing MAX(snapshot_ts) readers must see the whole completed chain after recovery. Apply equivalent complete-chain publication to ordinary realtime ingestion where needed to prevent a partial latest snapshot. --closing-only accepts an intended --session-date and a bounded close<=retrieval<close+30minutes recovery window, including early closes. Earlier non-closing launches explicitly skip; a missed required capture after cutoff is unavailable/incomplete, not a successful capture. Tests cover duplicate delivery, partial retry and delayed startup. Source completeness also rejects one-sided core ETF EOD chains and only skips verified complete snapshots; derive requested dates from completed Eastern sessions, not UTC date.today.


### Immutable revisions and replay selectors

Provenance on an overwritten row is insufficient for REQ-DATA-002. Every affected source-chain replacement and derived regeneration preserves the prior version and the new observation in append-only revision history before changing its current cache. Scope includes ETF EOD/realtime chains, earnings event chains, gamma_levels_eod and the gamma context repaired in strat feature tables, intraday_flow_15m, intraday_gex_15m and realtime_gex_15m. A typed journal or a shared generic journal is acceptable only if it stores the complete normalized values, dataset and natural/group key, revision identity, actual source retrieval clock, source session, input revision identities/hashes, immutable artifact/hash, provenance/configuration and explicit completeness/unavailability reason. A chain revision is one whole ticker/session or ticker/capture-slot group, not individually mixed contract revisions. A derived revision is keyed by the exact dataset/ticker/bucket or level group. Unavailable and corrected-removal revisions are retained too, so an older available value cannot silently survive a newer explicit unavailable observation.

History and corresponding current-cache rows, including complete-group markers, publish in the same group transaction. Rollback after any bounded COPY/write chunk leaves both history and cache unchanged. A complete-chain replacement records the exact contract membership; removed contracts cannot remain in the current group merely because an upsert did not visit them. Superseded financial observations remain immutable and replay-accessible. A separate append-only publication acknowledgement records the actual successful transaction-commit acknowledgement time; a journal revision without that acknowledgement is unavailable to strict as-of reads. A failed acknowledgement is an explicit partial/failed invocation; recovery can acknowledge at its actual recovery clock, never backdate it to source-session time. This separates vendor retrieval time from the time the persisted result was first confirmed available, without pretending a transaction-start clock was its commit clock.

The production strict selector accepts dataset/key, source session and explicit UTC as_of. It chooses the latest acknowledged revision with available_at <= as_of, enforces the required exact prior exchange session and input-basis/completeness contract, and returns typed unavailable when no eligible revision exists. It never falls back to a current mutable row, a future revision, another prior session, or an earlier available revision after a newer unavailable revision. Historical/replay consumers in the affected options and research paths must use this selector and propagate the same cutoff through source chains, minute bars, daily spot/rates and derived inputs; a sibling source without eligible immutable history makes the result unavailable, not guessed. Current-serving caches remain an explicit separate current-revision mode.

Legacy cache values can be seeded only as a legacy_unknown revision at their actual migration/acknowledgement clock. Old snapshot_date, EOD 23:00 marker and computed_at alone do not prove the observation was known earlier. Do not reconstruct unavailable historical knowledge by backdating a seed. A preserved legacy value becomes eligible only at/after the migration clock, with its unknown prior provenance visible. An explicitly source-backed earlier immutable observation may qualify through its recorded availability evidence; legacy rows without it do not.

Acceptance freezes an original chain/derived value, repairs it to a different source-backed value and asserts the original at its pre-repair eligible cutoff and the replacement at its post-repair cutoff. It also asserts unavailable before a legacy seed's knowledge clock, refusal when only a future revision exists, latest-unavailable propagation, exact prior-session gaps, whole-group history/cache rollback and no stale contract membership. Regenerated historical research datasets are labeled current-revision research, not historical point-in-time evidence; no model/artifact promotion or claims of replay validity follow from their refresh. Point-in-time mode requires the strict eligible revision path for every input and is separately tested.

### Explicit dependencies and storage safety

All affected earnings-date selection, historical fetch bounds, realtime RTH/closing guards and builders use the fail-closed `gcp.fetchers.evaluate_ew_strikes.nyse_sessions` NYSE schedule primitive. A missing calendar dependency or invalid schedule is an error; remove the historical-options weekday fallback. Tests cover 2026-11-27 early close, a 2027 holiday and DST. No second holiday list is introduced.

Option strikes and spot inputs must share as-traded, unadjusted units at the source session. Daily spot is canonical raw OHLC. Current intraday AV ingestion may have requested adjusted data: do not label those historical rows raw or repair GEX across an unverified split. The DATA market-data spec owns explicit `adjusted=false` intraday requests, raw-basis provenance and a bounded source-verified repair; this stream rejects unknown/mismatched basis at corporate-action boundaries and records unavailable output until source parity is verified. A split-date fixture proves equal spot/strike units and forbids double adjustment. A basis requirement is enforced by builder input validation, not assumed from column names.

On the large `etf_options_snapshots` table, add only a nullable closing-slot column in transactional schema. Build the slot/contract unique index outside `apply_schema.py` using `CREATE UNIQUE INDEX CONCURRENTLY ... WHERE capture_slot_ts IS NOT NULL`, after a read-only duplicate preflight; do not add a full-table ordinary UNIQUE constraint. Match the partial-index predicate in `ON CONFLICT`. The deployment runbook verifies index validity before enabling closing captures and specifies a lock timeout, failure recovery and rollback. Unit and local PostgreSQL tests prove duplicate-slot convergence; production rollout must record index duration and never claim a concurrent index ran inside the transactional schema command.

New earnings snapshots carry explicit event_reported_date. Existing legacy rows remain visible through the exact existing NULL-only `snapshot_date+1` view fallback while a bounded migration verifies each mapping against reported earnings date and the shared prior-session calendar. An ambiguous legacy mapping remains unknown; it is not rewritten by guess. The selector does not skip a group merely because legacy rows exist. A before/after event-count and ATM-value regression proves no loss of previously verified coverage and validates Monday/holiday corrected groups.

Derived flow/GEX tables retain source snapshot/session/as-of, pipeline identifier/version, safe configuration hash, rule/model identifier (explicit not-applicable where no model), source artifact/hash and code revision. These fields are additive; old rows have unknown provenance until bounded regeneration. Each entry-point invocation emits durable `record_job_run` run identity, input/output counts, watermark, duration, error class, retry count and terminal complete/partial/empty/skipped/failed status. A failure before the first group is recorded; telemetry delivery failures are explicit nonzero, with structured local logs preserving failure evidence.

The closing schedule is a deployment prerequisite in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-feat-deploy-001-av-deployment.md`: a dedicated `--closing-only` job invoked at 13:00–16:00 hourly America/New_York, guarded by actual NYSE close, with 600-second timeout and zero retries. This stream's code can merge before deployment, but closing production completion requires the deployed scheduler verifier and a complete slot smoke. Served earnings options are retained because earnings_event_outcomes is an active production materialized-view/API dependency. Restore a bounded recurring producer using the existing earnings-options-backfill job at 00:30 America/New_York Tuesday–Saturday (`30 0 * * 2-6`), after the previous weekday earnings-history run at19:15 and required vendor prior-session chain availability, with the existing32,400-second task timeout and zero automatic retries. A late invocation after settlement does not relabel missed due work healthy or backdate its availability. Its CLI is `python -m gcp.fetchers.fetch_av_earnings_options_backfill --lookback-days=30 --limit=100`: select due events within the recent 30-calendar-day Eastern window, from all configured earnings-universe tickers (currently 1,239; count is observed, not a hardcoded symbol restriction). --since/--until are bounded explicit repair overrides; conflicting absolute/relative windows are rejected. One indexed batched eligibility query resolves expected events, their exact prior NYSE sessions, verified source/event completion and already-completed groups before any API call. Do not restrict to 50 symbols or assume 100 selected events is the entire served universe.

Each invocation persists a source-backed immutable scope/window manifest containing universe version/hash and count, due expected events, eligible/excluded reasons, verified complete event/source revision identities, newly completed counts, pending/backlog counts and actual production availability clocks. The 100-event and API-call budgets are explicit: when more due eligible events remain, publish durable verified groups and return partial with the remaining backlog and deterministic next-shard cursor; never return complete/healthy merely because LIMIT100 was reached. Re-run/shard the pending set without refetching verified complete groups. Vendor unavailability and unknown mapping remain pending/unavailable and retain reasons. OPS owns the batched expected-event/prior-session completeness read, compares the entire due scope with verified completion records, and alerts on missing due events after the 04:00 producer settled window using its existing alert channel. Data owner is TeneikaAskew/project owner; recovery instructions name this bounded CLI and pending-shard manifest. DEPLOY must verify the recurring existing-job schedule and OPS prerequisite before served earnings-options recovery is reported complete. On-demand research flow/GEX stays historical-only, with no unsupported daily-age promise. Dependency checks must be satisfied before reporting overall plan completion.

### Capacity and cost

These are conservative design bounds, not measured production claims. The planner reports actual selected counts/bytes before mutation and refuses or re-shards a group exceeding a row/byte/latency envelope; it never truncates source data to fit. Core repair default is at most 30 completed NYSE sessions per SPY/IWM/QQQ ticker, processed sequentially one ticker/session group at a time; earnings default is at most 100 events in an explicit completed-session date window. Served earnings producer scans eligibility for the complete configured universe in one indexed query, while mutations/API requests are capped at 100 events; overflow is partial with explicit pending-shard state. No unbounded --backfill or year/quarter/full-retained-table scan is reachable from this repair runbook. At 0.5–2 seconds per production DB round trip, the estimates below use 2 seconds plus explicit transfer/compute allowances. Journal and cache writes share one staged streaming COPY and set-based inserts in the group transaction; bounded client COPY chunks do not create per-contract SQL calls. Publication acknowledgements and terminal telemetry are included in the stated query envelopes. Required indexes cover dataset/ticker/session or bucket and available_at; query-plan tests and production EXPLAIN verification reject a full retained-table scan. If estimates or smoke measurements exceed one quarter of the assigned task timeout, reduce session/event shard size or change the I/O shape before execution, rather than silently raising the timeout.
