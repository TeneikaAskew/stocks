# Correct point-in-time options dependencies and closing-session capture

## Authorization and intake

This records the scope of the six-stage remediation plan presented after the live AV/GCP audit and authorized by the user on 2026-10-12: "Proceed with the plan, executing inn parallel". Execution method: parallel isolated worktrees, one author and PR per capability. Capability: FEAT-OPTION-001; matching catalog row in docs/product/02-FEATURE-CATALOG.md. The listed issues are touched, not claimed closed without their own acceptance evidence.

## Problem

IWM/SPY October9 feature gamma_flip is NULL while QQQ is populated; this needs source-backed diagnosis rather than fabricated filling. Flow/GEX stores stopped June4–5, and historical earnings options stopped May14 despite a production materialized-view consumer. Earnings T-1 chain logic uses calendar days, which fails Mondays and holidays.

## Non-goals

No expansion to all 128 APIs, change to strategy/model promotion or risk calibration, fabricated financial values, blind rewrite of historical timestamps, unrelated IAM/audience changes, or unbounded backfill. API failures and historical-only exclusions remain explicit.

## Approaches considered

1. Targeted contract repair, independent reviewed streams, bounded source-backed repair and selective deployment — chosen to preserve the verified working paths.
2. Replace the complete ingestion platform — excessive scope and operational risk.
3. Change schedules without repairing data contracts — leaves the demonstrated corruption and observability gaps.

## Design

Trace gamma missingness through prior-session chain, actual crossing, rates and builder joins. Preserve legitimate no-crossing NULLs with explicit availability semantics; repair only a demonstrated parser/join/computation defect and coordinate its freshness expectation. Correct required flow/GEX builders to filter minute interval, valid unexpired contracts and exact prior trading session with explicit gap handling; refresh only bounded consumer-required slices and classify historical-only stores without unnecessary new recurring jobs. Use existing shared exchange calendar for earnings T-1 snapshots. Add event_reported_date provenance to new earnings-chain rows and update earnings_event_outcomes joins to the exact event/session mapping, preserving existing verified legacy coverage. Add bounded --since/--limit recent-event backfill and safe completeness checks. Add shared-calendar realtime RTH guard. Closing captures use deterministic capture_slot_ts=actual NYSE session close plus a unique slot/contract key; snapshot_ts records actual vendor retrieval time only; it is never the persisted available_at clock. Publish a complete validated ticker chain and its slot-completion marker atomically in one database transaction across bounded chunks; on failure roll back that entire ticker chain. Partial recovery is across whole tickers, not mixed contract retrieval timestamps within one ticker. Completed ticker slots skip duplicate delivery; unfinished tickers can retry and publish a full chain at one actual retrieval timestamp. Existing MAX(snapshot_ts) readers must see the whole completed chain after recovery. Apply equivalent complete-chain publication to ordinary realtime ingestion where needed to prevent a partial latest snapshot. --closing-only accepts an intended --session-date and a bounded close<=retrieval<close+30minutes recovery window, including early closes. Earlier non-closing launches explicitly skip; a missed required capture after cutoff is unavailable/incomplete, not a successful capture. Tests cover duplicate delivery, partial retry and delayed startup. Source completeness also rejects one-sided core ETF EOD chains and only skips verified complete snapshots; derive requested dates from completed Eastern sessions, not UTC date.today.


### Immutable revisions and replay selectors

Provenance on an overwritten row is insufficient for REQ-DATA-002. Every affected source-chain replacement and derived regeneration preserves the prior version and the new observation in append-only revision history before changing its current cache. Scope includes ETF EOD/realtime chains, earnings event chains, gamma_levels_eod and the gamma context repaired in strat feature tables, intraday_flow_15m, intraday_gex_15m and realtime_gex_15m. A typed journal or a shared generic journal is acceptable only if it stores the complete normalized values, dataset and natural/group key, revision identity, actual source retrieval clock, source session, input revision identities/hashes, immutable artifact/hash, provenance/configuration and explicit completeness/unavailability reason. A chain revision is one whole ticker/session or ticker/capture-slot group, not individually mixed contract revisions. A derived revision is keyed by the exact dataset/ticker/bucket or level group. Unavailable and corrected-removal revisions are retained too, so an older available value cannot silently survive a newer explicit unavailable observation.

History and corresponding current-cache rows, including complete-group markers, publish in the same group transaction. Rollback after any bounded COPY/write chunk leaves both history and cache unchanged. A complete-chain replacement records the exact contract membership; removed contracts cannot remain in the current group merely because an upsert did not visit them. Superseded financial observations remain immutable and replay-accessible. A separate append-only publication acknowledgement records the actual successful transaction-commit acknowledgement time; a journal revision without that acknowledgement is unavailable to strict as-of reads, and its confirmed durable claim fence blocks older acknowledged fallback. A failed acknowledgement is an explicit partial/failed invocation; recovery can acknowledge at its actual recovery clock, never backdate it to source-session time. This separates vendor retrieval time from the time the persisted result was first confirmed available, without pretending a transaction-start clock was its commit clock.

The actual shared producer/publisher recovery state machine is binding: a committed whole-group completion marker without an acknowledgement is COMMITTED_PENDING_ACK, never a completed skip. On retry, inspect its exact revision/membership/payload/artifact/hash/count/configuration and committed group identity through the indexed complete-state lookup BEFORE any vendor request or new revision allocation. If those existing facts are coherent and the current intended group is that revision, write acknowledgement for the SAME revision_id/revision_seq at the actual recovery clock and reuse the complete chain. Do not refetch/rewrite source, allocate a replacement revision/payload, backdate available_at or consume a second logical group quota merely to repair this acknowledgement gap. An acknowledged marker is the ordinary idempotent complete skip. If evidence is missing/incoherent, return explicit unavailable/partial and preserve records, never fabricate an acknowledgement. Late recovery of an older committed revision cannot supersede a newer group sequence. A failure after acknowledgement but before caller receipt converges to the same existing immutable acknowledgement on retry, without replacement. Tests inject process death/exception EXACTLY after group transaction commit and before acknowledgement for actual closing, regular realtime and grid on-demand publisher paths; then invoke their real retry entrypoint, assert zero additional AV requests, no second revision/payload/quota charge, unchanged membership/cache, actual later available_at and strict unavailability between commit and recovered acknowledgement. Hand-seeding a publication row in a pure selector fixture does not satisfy this producer test. Recovery lookup/ack is included within the existing <=10 group SQL and whole-run deadlines.

The production strict selector accepts dataset/key, source session and explicit aware UTC as_of. First inspect the latest relevant durable group claim/fence as described below, not only acknowledged revision rows. A confirmed pending replacement claim at/before as_of blocks an older acknowledged value; any visible possibly-newer claim whose knowledge clock is uncertain returns typed uncertain_claim_knowledge rather than guessing when its transaction committed. Once that claim has a coherent matching revision/publication with available_at<=as_of, select its greatest eligible semantic sequence BEFORE status/completeness. No future claim with a confirmed later knowledge clock affects an earlier cutoff. No eligible acknowledged revision, required exact-session mismatch, pending acknowledgement, unavailable/removed/legacy-unknown source or missing input history returns typed unavailable. Never fall back to a current mutable row, future revision, another session or older value behind a blocking newer fence. Apply the identical fence/sequence selector to source chains, derived revisions, event/terminal manifests and weekly publication identities; current-serving mode cannot bypass a pending replacement either.

Each canonical `(dataset,ticker,group_key)` has a positive BIGINT sequence allocated under one serialized group claim lock BEFORE source admission or current-cache mutation. The durable claim fixes revision_id/revision_seq and idempotency identity; rollback of the later group transaction cannot recycle its sequence. Revision and acknowledgement copy that exact sequence; unique `(dataset,ticker,group_key,revision_seq)` and matching publication_seq enforce semantic order. Failed/aborted/source-unavailable claims remain explicit states with actual observation clocks; never manufacture a complete revision or backdate publication. After enforcing the claim fence and available_at<=as_of, greatest publication_seq wins before status/completeness. Late recovery of an old claim cannot supersede a newer group. Equal-clock complete/unavailable, concurrent writers, late old acknowledgement, failed group transaction and unknown claim-clock fixtures exercise this exact order. The earlier phrasing that only acknowledged rows are ranked is superseded by the claim fence; an acknowledged seq1 is not returned through a pending seq2 gap.

The attainable commit-gap contract uses a conservative acknowledged pre-mutation fence, not a fictitious exact PostgreSQL commit timestamp. Add `av_options_group_claims(claim_id UUID PRIMARY KEY,dataset TEXT NOT NULL,ticker TEXT NOT NULL,group_key JSONB NOT NULL,revision_id UUID NOT NULL UNIQUE,revision_seq BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK(revision_seq>0),idempotency_key TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE,lease_token UUID NOT NULL,lease_generation BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK(lease_generation>0),lease_expires_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,state TEXT NOT NULL CHECK(state IN ('reserved','inflight','committed_pending_ack','published','aborted')),reservation_id UUID,configuration_sha256 TEXT NOT NULL)` with unique `(dataset,ticker,group_key,revision_seq)` and latest-group index. Operational lease/state may change only by checked owner/generation compare-and-swap; financial revisions remain immutable. Add append-only `av_options_group_claim_publications(claim_id UUID PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES av_options_group_claims(claim_id),claim_known_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL)`. Commit the claim/reservation first, capture claim_known_at only AFTER that transaction's actual acknowledgement, then insert its immutable claim-publication record before the vendor/group transaction. No source request/cache mutation starts before both acknowledgements. Claimed sequence may conservatively make a value unavailable while its later transaction is still in flight: this is explicitly pending observation, not a claim that financial data committed at that clock. If a crash occurs after claim commit but before claim-clock publication, visible uncertain claim state fails closed for affected group/cutoffs until reconciliation; it never uses retrieval/start/reserved_at as commit evidence. Recovery confirms the existing claim and publishes its actual later claim clock without backdating. At a confirmed future claim cutoff older historic values remain eligible; at a confirmed pending fence or uncertain claim the older fallback is blocked. A COMMITTED_PENDING_ACK revision uses the SAME claimed sequence, coherent membership/payload/artifact and actual later publication acknowledgement. Reconciliation of an aborted claim publishes an explicit unavailable/aborted assessment at its actual acknowledged clock or starts a new higher claim, never silently erases the fence. No assumption about track_commit_timestamp, WAL access or an unavailable exact crash-time commit clock is permitted. Strict replay deliberately reports conservative unknown during an unproven claim interval. Claims older than a newer confirmed eligible acknowledged sequence cannot invalidate that newer sequence merely because their own old publication was lost; fixed latest-sequence predicates enforce this without an all-history claim scan. Actual PostgreSQL tests keep seq1 acknowledged, claim seq2, delay its transaction across a cutoff, commit then kill before ack, and retry at a later clock; assert no seq1 fallback during the known pending/uncertain fence, no future revision early, no refetch/new revision/quota charge on coherent recovery, and exact before/after-clock results.

Legacy cache values can be seeded only as a legacy_unknown revision at their actual migration/acknowledgement clock. Old snapshot_date, EOD 23:00 marker and computed_at alone do not prove the observation was known earlier. Do not reconstruct unavailable historical knowledge by backdating a seed. A preserved legacy value can be inspected as typed legacy_unknown only at/after the migration clock, with its unknown prior provenance visible; it is not a verified complete strict-math input merely because migration finished. An explicitly source-backed earlier immutable observation may qualify through its recorded availability evidence; legacy rows without it do not.

Acceptance freezes an original chain/derived value, repairs it to a different source-backed value and asserts the original at its pre-repair eligible cutoff and the replacement at its post-repair cutoff. It also asserts unavailable before a legacy seed's knowledge clock, refusal when only a future revision exists, latest-unavailable propagation, exact prior-session gaps, whole-group history/cache rollback and no stale contract membership. Regenerated historical research datasets are labeled current-revision research, not historical point-in-time evidence; no model/artifact promotion or claims of replay validity follow from their refresh. Point-in-time mode requires the strict eligible revision path for every input and is separately tested.
