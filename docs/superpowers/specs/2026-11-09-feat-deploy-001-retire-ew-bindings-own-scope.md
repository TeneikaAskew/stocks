# Retire the EarningsWhispers credential bindings from the calendar job (EW-only scope)

## Problem

The approved `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-feat-deploy-001-retire-ew-bindings.md` (blob `5d2398111fc7ac658d79259a37ea3850490d1851`, verified on main `02f6df9b5b373c0f65cb3756436e3ad4977aaab6`) supersedes the 2026-10-12 AV deployment spec and carries its 27 done_when items verbatim, keeping them "in force" (its item 33). Its EarningsWhispers (EW) implementation, stocks#1659, changes only the EW bindings of `fetch-earnings-calendar`. A PR bound to a spec must tick every done_when item before Ready, so #1659 cannot become Ready until AV deployment work that has no PR, plan or owner delivers items 1 to 27.

## Non-goals

- No change to `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-feat-deploy-001-retire-ew-bindings.md`. It stays approved and unchanged, and every one of its 34 items stays in force as that spec's acceptance. This spec does not supersede, amend, narrow or waive it.
- No AV deployment work, no change to retry, sizing, schedule, source CLI, news bodies or AV secret references.
- No Cloud Run job, scheduler or secret mutation; no deployment; no credential read, rotation or deletion.
- No gate, hook, workflow, verifier, renderer, policy or template change.

## Approaches considered

1. Keep #1659 bound to the bindings spec: it then becomes Ready only when items 1 to 27 are true on main, which needs AV deployment work with no owner, or #1659 itself delivering all of it.
2. Supersede the bindings spec and split it into two new specs: refused. Items would lose their active home between admissions, the gate accepts one `supersedes` path per spec and refuses an already superseded target (round eight), and the carried CAT text forbids a two-new-spec supersession workaround.
3. Rebind #1659's existing plan to this spec inside a merge of main (revision 1): withdrawn. The gate's `plan_stays_bound` comment ("round five: re-pointing the plan at a weaker approved spec of the same FEAT swapped its contract") states the intent this would sidestep, and the bindings spec says "If a plan already exists on actualBASE, originalspec/branch/PR binding is immutable and normalfreshplan/branch needed, not in-CODErebind". That condition does not literally fire, because #1659's plan exists only on its branch, not on main; the withdrawal follows the stated intent of both texts rather than a literal refusal.
4. Chosen and admitted: one additional approved spec for the same FEAT, `supersedes: null`, carrying the EW items verbatim and the EW-scoped share of every shared safeguard at full strength, implemented by a re-cut: a fresh plan, branch and PR bound to this spec from the first commit.
