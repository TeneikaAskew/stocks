# Preserve AV daily adjustments and ingest selected reference endpoints

## Authorization and intake

This records the scope of the six-stage remediation plan presented after the live AV/GCP audit and authorized by the user on 2026-10-12: "Proceed with the plan, executing inn parallel". Execution method: parallel isolated worktrees, one author and PR per capability. Capability: FEAT-DATA-001; matching catalog row in docs/product/02-FEATURE-CATALOG.md. The listed issues are touched, not claimed closed without their own acceptance evidence.

## Problem

The production audit found 12,628 of 12,930 AV daily rows for October 5–9 without adjusted_close. The broad daily parser requests TIME_SERIES_DAILY_ADJUSTED but drops its adjusted close. INDEX_DATA and INDEX_CATALOG work with the production key, while no native index ingestion exists. Selected metadata endpoints have no ingestion contract.

## Non-goals

No expansion to all 128 APIs, change to strategy/model promotion or risk calibration, fabricated financial values, blind rewrite of historical timestamps, unrelated IAM/audience changes, or unbounded backfill. API failures and historical-only exclusions remain explicit.

## Approaches considered

1. Targeted contract repair, independent reviewed streams, bounded source-backed repair and selective deployment — chosen to preserve the verified working paths.
2. Replace the complete ingestion platform — excessive scope and operational risk.
3. Change schedules without repairing data contracts — leaves the demonstrated corruption and observability gaps.

## Design

Preserve adjusted_close in every AV daily writer. Add a bounded explicit-ticker/date repair that updates existing rows only, defaults to a reviewable dry-run, and commits per ticker with idempotent batch SQL. Add native SPX and VIX daily index routing preserving the existing ^VIX stored alias, raw OHLC and absent volume as NULL; retain explicit AV index provenance and reject silent Yahoo fallback. Add bounded INDEX_CATALOG, INDEX_DATA, ETF_PROFILE, DIVIDENDS, SPLITS and LISTING_STATUS ingestion using actual vendor contracts. Store normalized reference records and safe request/raw response/fetched UTC/SHA256 provenance in an additive SQL journal; limit journal retention to 90 days. Produce a 128-function registry with parameters, entitlement, symbol scope, timezone, table/key, cadence, owner, retained fields and excluded/historical-only reasons. Planned endpoint status is not called active until deployment evidence exists. Broad daily backfill and repair publish complete/partial/error counts and fail nonzero for failed requested groups. Registry format is version 1 with a functions mapping; lifecycle is one of active, planned, on_demand, historical_only or excluded, and keys are validated against declared table contracts. No imports from an unmerged sibling branch.

### Raw versus adjusted consumer policy

Canonical stored OHLCV remains as traded for session levels, option strikes and execution. Continuous daily returns, indicators and dimensionless cross-session STRAT labels/combos/FTFC use all OHLC consistently scaled by adjusted_close/close, never Close alone. Preserve canonical stored volume as traded, but build analysis Volume using cumulative split coefficients alone: for target basis date b, analysis_volume(t)=raw_volume(t) x product(split_coefficient(d), t<d<=b). A two-for-one split with 100 old shares and 200 new shares becomes 200/200 analysis shares; a dividend with no split leaves Volume unchanged. Never derive this volume factor from adjusted_close/close because that includes dividends. Retain required positive `split_coefficient` from AV daily field `8. split coefficient` in all relevant daily fetch/backfill paths and additive market_data_daily.split_coefficient DOUBLE PRECISION; indices use verified coefficient=1. Missing/unverified coefficient history makes split-sensitive RVOL/OBV unavailable, never assumes one. For a regeneration window, process price/volume on the same declared target-basis date; convert price-dimensional outputs to row raw-price units and share-dimensional OBV/volume averages back to that row's share basis using the independent split factor. Dimensionless RVOL stays continuous. Golden fixtures include split price 200/100 plus raw volume 100/200, and a dividend-only price adjustment whose volume factor remains one. `lib/price_basis.py` validates finite positive price and split factors, prepares adjusted daily price/analysis-volume frames and restores price-dimensional EMA/SMA/ATR/MACD/Bollinger and actionable STRAT trigger levels to the current row's raw units. Daily call sites in the live writer and historical indicator backfill invoke existing `lib.strat.StratClassifier` on adjusted frames; the generic classifier and intraday strategy fixtures stay unchanged. Previous-session executable levels remain explicit raw observations; a multi-bar structural trigger calculated in adjusted history is divided by the target session's validated factor, rather than taken from an unadjusted previous-session bar across a split. Tests compare labels and combos to an equivalent no-action continuous fixture, and verify that converted trigger levels match the target session's price units.

The daily loader defaults to `price_basis='raw'` for execution/levels and exposes `price_basis='adjusted'` only with explicit current-use semantics. Price-dimensional enrichment columns are rescaled consistently. Add `market_data_daily.indicator_basis_version TEXT`, `indicator_pipeline TEXT`, `indicator_artifact_id UUID` and `indicator_provenance JSONB`. Version `av-adjusted-daily-v1` covers the new continuous metrics and structural labels. Existing non-NULL enrichment is legacy: adjusted reads reject the legacy version and incomplete provenance until full-context regeneration recomputes it. Gap selection includes legacy-version rows even when all numeric fields are populated, with a maximum 50 tickers per run. Recent-window refresh certifies only rows it actually regenerates; older history remains legacy. A raw-close 200,100 split fixture must regenerate SMA2=100, not reinterpret legacy SMA2=150. No version is stamped on failed, skipped or partially calculated groups.

Historical/replay adjusted reads fail closed unless an immutable input vintage was recorded no later than the requested `as_of` and all required bars and enrichment can be selected from that same vintage. A mutable latest cache or a journal fetched after `as_of` never qualifies. This change does not implement a historical vintage reader: adjusted mode rejects year-scoped/historical requests, explicit past `as_of`, replay flags (`REPLAY_DATE`, `BRIEF_AS_OF`, `INSIGHT_AS_OF`) and attempts to use the current cache as an immutable artifact. Current analytics may examine historical bars using today's revision only when marked `current_revision_analytics`; this is refused by replay consumers and does not certify historical strategies. Regression tests place a later split revision behind an earlier as-of clock and require rejection. Raw replay behavior is unchanged, and #913 is touched without claiming complete historical corporate-action accounting.

Daily live and historical regeneration share a single basis/indicator/STRAT adapter and a declared context-window contract. For each target row, both compute from the same ordered 250 completed daily bars ending at that row (or all available bars when fewer), with identical split/dividend factors and indicator configuration; historical backfill must not silently use unlimited preceding context for recursive indicators while live uses 250. Processing is bounded by ticker/date batches and vectorized rolling/reusable prefix preparation where applicable, never one SQL query per row. A shared golden fixture passes through both actual call sites and compares every persisted daily indicator, STRAT label/combo, price/share unit restoration and basis/provenance marker exactly within declared floating precision. Intraday golden arithmetic remains unchanged. For a three-target smoke, one source-window SQL read covers the union needed by all targets; tests assert that I/O shape. Full-history regeneration shards explicit dates/tickers within its run budget rather than claiming unbounded recomputation deployability. Add --max-output-rows=500 across at most 50 tickers and a 600s deadline; reject a requested explicit shard exceeding the output cap at preflight and select automatic backlog by whole bounded ticker/date groups. Estimate 500 target rows x250-bar context x~160 bytes ~=20 MB conceptual compute input (overlapping source bars fetched once/ticker), <=50 source reads +50 input-artifact receipts +<=50 output batches, ~100s compute at 0.2s/target plus <=300s SQL/pacing overhead <=400s, under one quarter of the reviewed 2700s cap. Smoke must measure actual compute latency and reduce cap if it breaches budget; no unbounded all-history command is executable by the runbook.

### Storage, revision and provenance contracts

All new times below are UTC `TIMESTAMPTZ`; event dates are exchange-local `DATE`. Financial missing tokens (`null`, `None`, `n/a`, blank) remain SQL NULL. Invalid required identities/dates/finite financial fields fail their group; a malformed response does not erase valid rows. Each normalized reference table carries `source_journal_id UUID NOT NULL`, `fetched_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL`, and `provenance JSONB NOT NULL`. The provenance object requires `source`, `source_as_of`, `pipeline_version`, `configuration_sha256`, `rule_version`, `artifact_sha256`, and `code_revision`; deployment supplies a nonempty reviewed code SHA, and the configuration hash excludes secrets. Schema constraints and tests validate required metadata. `source_journal_id` references the immutable journal identity, not a free-standing hash.

Hash the original HTTP bytes for vendor responses, retaining both CSV bytes and normalized parsed rows for listings. For derived input, hash/store a deterministic source-frame artifact including ordered dates, raw OHLCV, adjusted close, source-row journal links and the compute configuration. Upsert uniqueness `(kind,function,symbol,fetched_at,artifact_sha256)` makes a repeated write of one fetched response idempotent; identities never change their original payload/hash metadata. Global catalog/listing requests use the stable non-NULL `*` symbol; repeated journal writes reuse the original fetched_at/UUID receipt. `tests/gcp/test_av_reference_data.py::test_global_receipt_retry_idempotent` proves one catalog/listing receipt survives a repeated write with no duplicate payload or NULL-key uniqueness gap.

Repaired adjusted-close/split-coefficient rows get `daily_source_journal_id` and `daily_provenance` tied to the corresponding coherent adjusted endpoint response. Before repair, verify response symbol/date identity, finite positive valid OHLC tuple (low<=min(open,close)<=max(open,close)<=high), exact integer volume, recognized AV daily source, and agreement of ALL stored raw OHLC/volume with the fetched daily source (only machine-roundoff tolerance, absolute <=1e-8 for price, no financial tolerance). Intraday-fallback sources, mismatched/revised vendor raw values, absent identities or nonfinite values are refused; do not mix fresh adjusted close with old raw close or guess a factor. Lock/recheck source tuple and updated_at in the per-ticker transaction before modifying adjustment/provenance fields, to reject concurrent changes. Any requested-row mismatch fails its bounded ticker group without changing that group's raw or adjusted values. Refused rows are counted/reported and require separate reviewed coherent-source replacement, not automatic correction. `tests/gcp/test_av_market_repair.py::test_daily_repair_refuses_incoherent_source` covers vendor OHLC revision, fallback mismatch, malformed tuple and concurrent change, with unchanged stored financial fields.

A native index source replacement that changes OHLC invalidates all continuous/STRAT features from the earliest changed session through the existing latest row for that ticker, because EMA and rolling/weekly dependencies extend beyond the changed row. In the same transaction set `indicator_basis_version=invalidated_index_source_v1`, clear indicator_artifact_id, and record the changed-source journal/window in indicator_provenance; all known continuous and structure fields become unavailable to both raw and adjusted loaders until bounded regeneration certifies them. Clearing or masking must include non-NULL legacy indicators and labels, not only NULL-gap cases. Canonical raw OHLC can still be served as newly sourced observations with their receipt, but stale derived fields are not exposed. The invalidation update is one date-bounded set operation per ticker, capped at 10,000 existing rows; larger histories require explicit shards before source promotion. Same-value source refresh does not invalidate features. `tests/gcp/test_av_reference_data.py::test_index_revision_invalidates_future_features` covers changed and unchanged OHLC, subsequent rolling rows, loader unavailability and successful regeneration before re-exposure. Regenerated indicators get `indicator_artifact_id` tied to their exact source-frame artifact and `indicator_provenance` with the required fields above, including source window and basis version. Legacy source rows lacking vendor journal links are explicitly identified in the captured source artifact, rather than assigned invented vendor provenance; computation is reproducible from that artifact without claiming original vendor observability. Per-ticker transactions publish the accepted journal receipt (`validation_status=validated`, rows_normalized equal to the committed normalized count) and update/upsert its normalized rows atomically. Eligible zero-event DIVIDENDS/SPLITS or an explicitly validated no-NYSE-session index window publishes `validation_status=validated_empty`, rows_normalized=0, in the same group transaction; catalog/listing/profile empty payloads are failed, not valid empty. A safely archived rejected response may persist as failed with rows_normalized=0 for diagnosis but never acts as a success receipt. Failed normalized storage rolls back the accepted receipt and that group; completed groups survive later failure. `tests/gcp/test_av_reference_data.py::test_validated_receipt_atomic_and_empty_eligibility` proves rollback, valid corporate-action emptiness and rejection of empty catalog/listing/profile; its failed/archive-only receipt case proves no false freshness advancement. Smoke SQL anti-joins must find zero missing artifact links or metadata fields among rows written by the smoke run.

After 90 days retire journal raw/parsed payloads, preserving identity/hash/provenance as a compact tombstone so normalized-row linkage remains valid. This is a 90-day raw-payload policy, not deletion of referenced journal identities. Indexed cleanup selects at most 100 expired payload-bearing rows per invocation, retires at most 80 MiB of combined raw_response + parsed_response payload bytes and uses at most four SQL transactions; at least one maximum-sized accepted receipt (20 MiB raw plus at most 20 MiB canonical parsed bytes, 40 MiB combined) always fits. Canonical parsed encoding is separately capped at 20 MiB before persistence. Cleanup retires both payload columns atomically per receipt and measures their combined bytes; tests accept and retire a 40 MiB receipt, prove convergence and preserve tombstone identities. Its backlog, rows and reclaimed bytes are telemetry. A cleanup failure is reported as partial and cannot hide ingestion success/failure. Immutable historical evidence is unavailable after payload retirement unless separately retained; no PIT claim follows from a tombstone.
