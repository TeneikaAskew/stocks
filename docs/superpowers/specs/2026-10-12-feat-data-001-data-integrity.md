## Design

### A. Source receipts and temporal integrity

Create `lib/source_receipts.py`, with immutable `SourceReceipt` and `SourceHealth` models; persistence in `gcp/source_receipts.py`; additive canonical schema in `gcp/schema.sql`, applied by the existing default `gcp/apply_schema.py`/API deploy path, with `gcp/queries/source_integrity_schema.sql` as a companion review artifact only. Test the actual applier against isolated PostgreSQL twice; readers cannot deploy before those tables/columns exist. Do not alter gate/trigger governance. Reuse existing database and shared exchange-calendar utilities. Centralize current-facing read decisions here rather than adding unrelated age heuristics to every consumer.

`SourceReceipt` fields: `run_id`, `source`, `dataset`, `partition_key`, `status` (`complete|partial|empty|failed|unavailable`), `requested_from`, `requested_to`, `observed_at`, `available_at`, `fetched_at`, `completed_at`, `rows_received`, `rows_valid`, `rows_written`, `rejected_count`, `watermark`, `error_class`, `http_error` (redacted endpoint, method, status), `reason`, `coverage` and `revision_id`, `attempted_calls`, `successful_calls`, `raw_receipt_uri`, `raw_payload_hash`, `rejection_reasons`, `field_completeness`, `latest_successful_fetch`, `effective_session_date` and `downstream_status`. Extend existing `job_runs` telemetry where possible; a receipt is a source/partition child of that run, not a competing definition of success. Retain redacted raw receipts or a content-addressed reference under the existing artifact policy so a future one-sided chain can be traced before versus after normalization. Timestamps are timezone-aware UTC or null; source-provided dates retain their precision metadata. Coverage describes requested and achieved ranges plus selected/processed/deferred ticker counts, never infers coverage from MAX(date). `observed_at` is the event/snapshot time, `available_at` is the earliest provable availability of this exact version to this system, and `fetched_at` is transport time. Use `fetched_at` as the canonical transport field in receipts, source_evidence_versions, API metadata and RFC8785 integrity input; it may be null when no transport clock is proven. `captured_at` is not a fourth source clock or an implicit alias for fetch/availability. A local section-capture operation may have its own capture argument, but cannot fill an unknown transport timestamp from that argument, generated_at or available_at. Test three deliberately distinct instants through persistence/API/hash serialization. A freshly fetched old quote is old. Unknown historical availability stays unknown; do not backdate repaired values to manufacture replay eligibility. An event scheduled for a future date can be known before cutoff; its released actual value needs its own availability/version.

`assess_source(receipt: SourceReceipt | None, *, now: datetime, cutoff: datetime | None, policy: FreshnessPolicy) -> SourceHealth` returns status (`current|stale|partial|empty|failed|unavailable`), reason, source clocks, coverage and recovery instruction. `eligible_at(observed_at: datetime | None, available_at: datetime | None, cutoff: datetime) -> bool` is for realized observations only; requires both known and no later than cutoff. Scheduled event metadata uses `available_at <= cutoff` independently of future event time. The acceptance fixture has a release scheduled for `2026-10-14T12:30:00Z`, schedule version available `2026-10-12T12:00:00Z`, replay cutoff `2026-10-12T13:00:00Z`, and actual version first available `2026-10-14T12:30:05Z`: preserve event identity/time and the earlier-known schedule fields, but return actual=null with its unavailable/not-yet-released reason. A later schedule revision is excluded too. Test both the hidden-schedule mutation (applying realized observed_at to the whole event) and leaked-actual mutation. These are declared deterministic fixtures, not new provider observations. Missing clocks never mean current. Per-dataset policies declare owner, cadence, threshold and recovery; preserve existing exchange-session rules for EOD and established gamma age tiers. Record partial/failed sources even if another source succeeds. Writes and receipts are idempotent by run/source/partition; publish success only after the data transaction commits. Retain last-good values with stale provenance on later failures.

### B. Options validity and recent repair

Create `lib/options_integrity.py`: `validate_chain(rows: DataFrame, *, ticker: str, session_date: date) -> ChainValidation` checks identity/date consistency, duplicates, supported option sides, nonnegative finite OI, at least one usable positive-OI contract on each required side, and finite Greeks where required by the consumer. All-zero OI on the whole chain or either required side is unavailable/insufficient_open_interest, not a sourced zero-exposure summary. A valid chain may contain individual zero-OI contracts; do not require every contract to be positive. Completeness requires both calls and puts with usable contracts; equal side counts are not required. Side presence is necessary, not a claim that every listed contract is present. All-zero/missing required Greeks are separately unavailable for gamma. Failed input has a reason and cannot publish directional gamma or synthetic zero totals.

Use the same validator from `gcp/fetchers/fetch_av_historical_options.py`, `gcp/research/p2_build_gamma_levels.py`, `lib/gamma.py` entry points, `lib/agents/summarizers.py`, and `platform/api/routers/options.py`. Skip-existing consults validated receipts/partition statistics rather than existence of one row. `--from-latest` revisits the last **5 exchange sessions** by default, inclusive of the newest completed session, to catch a recent hole behind the high watermark; an explicit date range remains available. Compute completed sessions from the shared NYSE exchange-calendar schedule and its actual market_close in timezone-aware UTC, never by subtracting weekdays or assuming 16:00 on half-days. For an injected clock just before `2026-11-27T18:00:00Z` (13:00 ET half-day close after Thanksgiving), expected ascending sessions are November 19, 20, 23, 24, 25; at/after that close they are November 20, 23, 24, 25, 27. November 26 holiday and an uncompleted November 27 session must never occupy a slot. Include the weekend continuation as the same last-completed set. If the exchange calendar is unavailable, this default five-completed-session mode reports unavailable/failed calendar coverage rather than falling back to weekdays and claiming five valid sessions. Calendar completion is not provider-data completeness; the selected partition still needs a valid source receipt. Bound provider retries to existing approved limits and show partial terminal status.

**Exact selected universe and bounded slices.** The captured deployed options command is --tickers "SPY IWM QQQ SPX" --from-latest; source TICKERS agrees. Persist those four selected identities (including SPX) and five exact completed session dates before validation. Production gamma currently selects SPY/IWM/QQQ; retain separate options coverage and downstream gamma-required subsets in the manifest. Do not drop SPX, infer its provider availability, or make it an initial historical repair target. With --tickers ALL, resolve the existing default-four union watchlist once, deduplicate and seal a deterministic universe/hash. Process at most4 ticker identities per slice, validating the rolling5 sessions plus the separately bounded older frontier described below (at most20 partition requests total across rolling and backlog, including bounded transport retry attempts; no hidden retry multiplier). Complete valid partitions require no HTTP; exhausted requests leave explicit pending/partial partitions. Persist selected/processed/deferred identities, exact per-partition outcomes, session list, cursor and continuation identity transactionally; round-robin continuation advances completed work without losing failed/deferred work, and newly added watchlist members enter the next versioned universe rather than mutating a sealed one. A run cannot claim whole-universe complete from one slice; no parent/current receipt releases a dependent whose required subset remains deferred. Do not launch extra scheduled jobs to drain a large ALL universe: resume within explicitly budgeted invocations or the next ordinary dispatch, retaining original session keys and truthful age/deferred coverage. Tests use >4 selected tickers including a watchlist member and SPX, stop/restart between slices, prove every selected ticker remains accounted for with eventual bounded progress and no silent starvation, and exercise failed SPX availability independently from the valid QQQ no-op control. No vendor availability is fabricated.

Stage and validate the complete replacement for one `(ticker, snapshot_date, data_source, market_session)` partition before an atomic publish; no intermediate calls-only partition is current. Do not delete last-good data when refetch fails. A repair manifest contains exact partitions, before/after counts by side, payload hashes, code version and receipt IDs; §L pins the signed numerical oracle and surface-specific tolerances. Initial explicit target is `IWM,SPY` on `2026-10-08`; QQQ is a no-op control. Provider refetch is a later revision: preserve original observation versions/audit references and prevent it appearing as known on October 8.

**Retained high-watermark backlog.** The rolling five completed sessions augment, not replace, existing high-watermark catch-up. Persist a per-ticker oldest unresolved exchange-session frontier and explicit failed/deferred partition keys. In each four-ticker slice validate the rolling20 keys in one grouped read, plus at most one older session per ticker from the durable backlog; share the existing20 total HTTP-attempt ceiling. When old work exists reserve up to4 attempts for its four keys and admit at most16 rolling fetch attempts; any unfetched rolling key stays partial and cannot release a dependent. Fairly rotate budget between pending rolling keys while always giving an eligible older frontier bounded progress. Do not advance a frontier past a failed/unvalidated partition or recompute it from MAX(date). A restarted invocation reuses its sealed keys; new wall-clock sessions cannot delete old backlog. A fixture misses12 completed sessions, includes holiday/half-day and a failed oldest partition, resumes across invocations and proves every missing partition remains tracked, eventually complete under successful fixtures, with<=20 attempts/run and no expansion of initial IWM/SPY October8 repair authorization.

**Raw-versus-adjusted basis.** Persist `price_basis` (`raw_as_traded|adjusted_common_basis|unknown`), corporate-action/factor version IDs and first availability, currency, contract multiplier/deliverable identity and spot-source basis in partition versions, dependency hashes and repair/derived-impact manifests. Options strike/premium/spot inputs to existing gamma are raw as traded on the same session and compatible standard100-share deliverable; never pair raw strikes with adjusted spot. Unknown/nonstandard deliverables or a known split whose compatible vintage cannot be proven are unavailable with `adjustment_basis_unproven`; do not apply an invented split factor or change the100-share numerical oracle. Cross-session full-distribution GEX/VEX/STRAT comparisons require recorded compatible basis/deliverable segments; for a split-crossing distribution lacking verified point-in-time common-basis inputs, fail the affected repair as unavailable rather than combine bases, silently freeze cuts or omit history. Reuse the separately approved FEAT-DATA-001 AV market-data price-basis helper once merged; do not duplicate its normalization or claim that current adjusted_close proves a historical factor. Split-boundary fixtures prove raw-strike+adjusted-spot rejection, unknown/late factor rejection before cutoff, a compatible raw same-session control, and explicit refusal of an unproven cross-split distribution while preserving the pinned October8 full-chain results.

### C. Bounded derived repair

Add optional `--start-date`, `--end-date`, `--as-of`, `--dry-run` and repair-manifest input to the existing production gamma builder, and bounded end-date/as-of support to the existing STRAT builder. Keep existing invocation behavior compatible. Expose `rebuild_partitions(manifest: RepairManifest, *, as_of: datetime, dry_run: bool) -> RepairResult` through `gcp/repair_data_integrity.py`; it orchestrates production builders, not a second financial implementation. The initial direct targets are October 8 `(IWM|SPY,date)` gamma and spot-dependent `daily_vex`, plus October 9 STRAT context joined from them. They are a required minimum, **not proof that only October 9 can change**. Production `_load_gamma_levels` reads full per-ticker history, `_compute_daily_vex` derives VEX from chain+gamma spot and skips cached dates, and `_compute_terciles` computes full-history GEX/VEX p33/p67 used by every output row. The manifest must therefore invalidate/recompute the exact repaired chain/spot VEX cache entries, evaluate before/after eligible full-distribution cutpoints and compare all potentially affected context classifications, not merely the direct prior-session join.

Before any writes, `plan_derived_impact(manifest: RepairManifest, *, as_of: datetime) -> DerivedImpact` records finite read bounds/counts and immutable version/hash inputs for the complete eligible gamma/VEX distribution, old/new spots and VEX values, old/new cutpoints, direct affected joins, and exact additional `(ticker,feature_table,timeframe,bar_date,ts)` keys/columns whose `gex_tercile`, `vex_tercile` or `dealer_regime` changes. Use production `_compute_terciles`, `total_vex` and `_add_context`; project existing feature context where sufficient rather than recomputing unrelated indicators/full raw bars. For quantile-shift candidates, bounded SQL range predicates may narrow rows, but compare using production bin-edge semantics and prove the query includes every changed classification, including edge equality, nulls and <30-value distributions. A manifest may assert October9-only writes **only with recorded evidence that cutpoints and non-target classifications remain unchanged**. Otherwise enumerate the exact additional impacted partitions/rows, partition the work to existing capacity bounds and retain a checksum for a proven unaffected partition. Do not freeze stale cutpoints to force locality, and do not rewrite all historical features indiscriminately.

Reads are bounded by explicit ticker/date/row/page budgets and the requested `as_of`; each chain, gamma, spot, VEX cache and distribution constituent must be an eligible immutable version with observed/available times no later than that boundary. Full-history here means the finite eligible distribution within the manifest, not all currently mutable rows or future data. A historical replay cannot consume later repaired input/cutpoint versions. Corrected-history output carries the actual regeneration availability and distribution dependency cutoff, which may be later than its historical bar date; it cannot masquerade as a point-in-time observation on that bar date. Persist the distribution input version/hash set and cutpoint recipe as a derived revision dependency so later replay sees the original eligible version or explicit unavailability. Never backdate that dependency proof. Include read-only indicator warmup only where needed, with separately stated read and write scopes.

Dry-run fails closed on unknown distribution coverage/availability, unbounded scan, unresolved cache version, unenumerated classification impacts or capacity overflow. Stage affected gamma/VEX/context revisions under the manifest and publish the derived-complete receipt/current revision only after every listed dependent partition completes; interruption leaves downstream degraded/resumable, not a mixed corrected distribution labeled complete. No unrelated consumer is released until that completion receipt. These requirements apply to explicit repair manifests and the scheduled runtime's repair branch; already-valid complete partitions remain no-ops.

Repaired analytical datasets are labeled with regeneration time and revision. A historical as-of run excludes later-fetched replacement chains unless a recorded prior version proves availability. Test both the historical result being unavailable and the explicitly labeled corrected-history result. No model training or calibration consumes these results as a validated baseline during this repair. Re-run convergence and an unrelated partition checksum are mandatory.
