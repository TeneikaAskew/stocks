## Problem

The approved `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-feat-catalyst-001-retire-ew-source.md` (blob `f08d08ab`, main 1fb97576) supersedes the 2026-10-13 AV catalysts delivery spec and keeps its 42 done_when items in force (its item 50). ## Approaches considered

1. Wait for #1430 and the unowned AV lanes.
2. Supersede the consolidated spec and split it into two new specs: forbidden by the consolidated text ("No deleted/weakened contract or two-new-spec supersession workaround is authorized") and by the gate's single-`supersedes` and round-eight rules.
3. Rebind #1681's existing plan inside a merge of main (revision 1): withdrawn.
