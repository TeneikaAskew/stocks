# Render the declared retry split as a trusted substitution

Status: draft. The owner approved the narrow scope on 2026-11-01 (02:35:06 UTC): admit only the derived retry-split substitutions, keep every independent truth check and the fail-closed rejection of unrelated prose. The owner accepted six done_when items as written at 78dde0bd on 2026-11-01 (stocks#1660). A local review of #1660's head 591476b6 found that they own only §11's split while §6 states the same split, so the first four items are amended here (§6 list shape, rendered-document checks, narrowed predecessor reference); items 5 and 6 are unchanged. The amended items await the owner's written acceptance.

## Problem

`check_generated_docs.gate_derived_numbers` derives the job retry split from `gcp/deploy.sh` and requires 05-a's prose to match it. Its own docstring says such numbers should never be a prose claim anyone keeps in sync. The REQ-GOV-004 render gate accepts a feature branch's 05-a only as main's trusted renderer re-renders it from merge-base prose, and `insert_blocks(counts=True)` owns only `RUNTIME_RELATION_COUNT`, `RELATION_BREAKDOWN` and `ASOF_LABELS`. 05-a states the split twice: §6 (line 346 on main: "`--max-retries 0` for 43 of the 68 declared jobs, `1` for 24 and `2` for one") and §11 (line 811: "`--max-retries 0` is the norm (43 of the 68 declared jobs); the 24 `--max-retries 1` jobs"), and gate_derived_numbers reads both. A declared retry change therefore fails one of the two checks whichever way either line is left.

Observed on the FEAT-DEPLOY-001 news branch (base d806bfd1, source f3206696, probe 44895970): the PR gate refused the computed 46/21 prose ("changes line 811 outside its inventory blocks"), and with main's 43/24 prose, six test_check_generated_docs.py checks failed; main passes 140/140. The EW evaluator retirement (stocks#1658, head 9b076797) hits the same gap from the other side: removing one retries-1 job declares 67 jobs, 43 at retries 0 and 23 at retries 1, and its CI stops at test_committed_docs_pass_every_gate. This is the same contradiction class the predecessor resolved for relation totals and breakdowns. REQ-GOV-004 admits declared job retry-split counts since 27c3c9ec (#1655).

## Non-goals

No deletion, historicizing or rewording of retry counts; no removed or loosened assertion; no owned-FEAT exception; no change to the render-check comparison, `scripts/gate/spec_gate.py` verdict logic, the hook, gate workflows, pinned copies or the committed snapshot; no new document, marker, live input or as-of semantics; no generated-document change in this PR; no retry policy change to any job; no change to the supersedes protocol (separately diagnosed).

## Approaches considered

1. Hand-edit the prose in the feature PR: refused by REQ-GOV-004 and rightly so, since a typed number is what the derived check exists to catch.
2. Remove the counts from the prose: rejected by the owner; it hides a checked fact instead of rendering it.
3. Render the split in the trusted renderer from the declared jobs, as the predecessor did for relation counts. Chosen: the number is computable from the same parse the check already uses, the outside-prose comparison stays fail-closed, and only the exact computed digits can move.

## Design

Retained predecessor contract: the Design, File ownership limits not changed below, Capacity and cost, and Validation and rollout sections of `2026-10-13-feat-cicd-001-complete-generated-inventory.md` remain operative word for word, except that its sentence "Existing substitutions are exactly the unchanged renderer RUNTIME_RELATION_COUNT, RELATION_BREAKDOWN and ASOF_LABELS" becomes "Existing substitutions are exactly the renderer's RUNTIME_RELATION_COUNT, RELATION_BREAKDOWN, ASOF_LABELS and RETRY_SPLIT", and its renderer-unchanged clause admits only the RETRY_SPLIT addition below. Its named bootstrap prerequisites are on main: the requirement-only change #1440 (8322eef3), the source-review policy spec #1461 (ddb1e6d7) and its implementation #1464 (fdbcdaa7, `.claude/skills/product-delivery/SKILL.md`), the source-review verifier spec #1462 (182d444e) and its implementation #1470 (7e5cb49e, `scripts/maintenance/doc_source_snapshot.py`, the producer and verifier generation the SITE note and sidecar use), and the predecessor's own implementation #1538 (a5749d4c). Change: add `RETRY_SPLIT` beside the existing substitutions in `scripts/maintenance/doc_inventory.py`, matching only three literal shapes: §11's two on main's 05-a line 811 and §6's list on line 346, "`--max-retries <k>` for <n> of the <total> declared jobs, `<k>` for <n> and `<k>` for <c>". Rendering replaces each `<n>` with `Counter(job["max_retries"])[<k>]`, `<total>` with the declared job count from `repo_inventory`, which is the parse `gate_derived_numbers` counts, defaults included, and `<c>` the same way. A count keeps the form of the merge-base token it replaces: §6's last count is the word "one" on main, so it renders as the `check_generated_docs.WORD_NUMBERS` word for the new count ("two" for 2), or digits above twelve, the table's limit; every other count is digits. The flag digits, every other byte and clause order are preserved, and the list never gains or loses a term: a retry value no term names is outside this substitution. Scope is 05-a only. `insert_blocks(counts=True)` applies it; `restore_blocks` keeps `counts=False`, so a model edit after the monthly render is still reported. `check_generated_docs` is unchanged; the tests instead prove each owned per-flag count is read by `gate_derived_numbers` and that every fixture's rendered 05-a passes it. `gate_derived_numbers` does not read either `<total>` (on main a hand-typed 69 in either shape passes it), so the totals are pinned by the render tests only; this spec does not add a total check. Other phrasings it parses stay checked and non-owned.

Not owned: §6's opening reconciliation, "76 jobs exist live; 68 are declared ... 66 names appear on both sides, so 10 ... and 2 declared jobs are not deployed", and its name lists. The declared total there is computable, but the other figures and names come from the live snapshot and the hand-written history, and rendering the one declared term alone would break the sentence's own arithmetic (for the EW retirement, 67 declared beside 66 + 2). No gate reads it. A declared-set change such as the EW retirement leaves that sentence to the next live render and its reviewed refresh, as today; this spec neither fixes nor loosens it.

The gate needs no code change: its RENDER_MODE already runs main's `insert_blocks(counts=True)` over merge-base prose. Once this lands, a feature branch that changes a declared retry carries the exact computed split, and nothing else outside the blocks may differ.

## Risks

Sequencing: the predecessor's status-only flip was refused on main until the supersedes-chain gate implementation (stocks#1656) merged as cb136f3e; this spec and the predecessor retirement land together in this PR.
