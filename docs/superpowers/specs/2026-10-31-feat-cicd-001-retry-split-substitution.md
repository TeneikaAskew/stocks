## Problem

`check_generated_docs.gate_derived_numbers` derives the job retry split from `gcp/deploy.sh` and requires 05-a's prose to match it. Its own docstring says such numbers should never be a prose claim anyone keeps in sync. The REQ-GOV-004 render gate accepts a feature branch's 05-a only as main's trusted renderer re-renders it from merge-base prose, and `insert_blocks(counts=True)` owns only `RUNTIME_RELATION_COUNT`, `RELATION_BREAKDOWN` and `ASOF_LABELS`. 05-a states the split twice: §6 (line 346 on main: "`--max-retries 0` for 43 of the 68 declared jobs, `1` for 24 and `2` for one") and §11 (line 811: "`--max-retries 0` is the norm (43 of the 68 declared jobs); the 24 `--max-retries 1` jobs"), and gate_derived_numbers reads both. A declared retry change therefore fails one of the two checks whichever way either line is left. One test also pins the split itself: `test_the_retry_split_must_match_deploy_sh` replaces main's literal "`--max-retries 0` for 43 of the 68 declared jobs, `1` for 24" and asserts a finding, so once a branch re-renders §6 the replace does nothing and the test fails; `test_a_spelled_out_retry_count_is_validated` does the same with "`2` for one" whenever the retries-2 count changes. A consumer cannot adapt either test beside its 05-a: the gate refuses 05-a changing alongside tests/scripts/test_check_generated_docs.py ("which decide the render; land that change first, then re-render"), so the adaptation belongs to this change.

Observed on the FEAT-DEPLOY-001 news branch (base d806bfd1, source f3206696, probe 44895970): the PR gate refused the computed 46/21 prose ("changes line 811 outside its inventory blocks"), and with main's 43/24 prose, six test_check_generated_docs.py checks failed; main passes 140/140. The EW evaluator retirement (stocks#1658, head 9b076797) hits the same gap from the other side: removing one retries-1 job declares 67 jobs, 43 at retries 0 and 23 at retries 1, and its CI stops at test_committed_docs_pass_every_gate. This is the same contradiction class the predecessor resolved for relation totals and breakdowns. REQ-GOV-004 admits declared job retry-split counts since 27c3c9ec (#1655).

## Risks

A broad pattern could rewrite historical or unrelated retry mentions: the literal shapes and the 05-a-only scope, refused by the third done_when item's controls, prevent that. A drift between render and check shapes, or a statement of the split the render does not own, would re-create the contradiction: the first item requires every fixture's fully rendered 05-a to pass `gate_derived_numbers`, and the second requires each owned shape to be read by it. A test pinned to the current numbers would block every consumer after this lands, which is why the second item adapts the two that exist and requires the consumer suites on every fixture. A fixture read from git history would be missing in CI's shallow checkout, which is why the fixtures are committed and hash-checked. A total its terms contradict would pass every check, which is why the render raises instead. A silent correction of a model's wrong number: restore_blocks keeps counts=False. Sequencing: the predecessor's status-only flip was refused on main until the supersedes-chain gate implementation (stocks#1656) merged as cb136f3e; this spec and the predecessor retirement land together in this PR. Merging this spec with the predecessor left approved, or flipping it later in a separate change, is not an authorized procedure.
