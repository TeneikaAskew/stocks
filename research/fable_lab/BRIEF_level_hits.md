would be a call or the next levels, vs just was it a call. A call can run a minute and reverse, not helpful. I
mean was it a call that ran even 5 minutes or more at some point of the day hitting the levels. The 10 am issue is
a concern but understanding if it meets Rob Smith's The Strat is something to consider as well. Is it tapping the
levels of the time frames."

   it did; before vs after 10:00?
5. Report days (CPI, jobs, FOMC, PCE) vs other days.
Per ticker and combined, both windows, with ranges. Worked example days with charts (1-min price, trigger,
targets, Strat levels, the 5-minute hold marked). Suggest rule changes as hypotheses for the triggers_levels lane.
